# Hospital image assets

The bundled images are illustrative images for the fictional seeded clinics, generated using the built-in `image_gen` tool via the imagegen skill. They are not verified photos of real healthcare providers. Uploaded hospital photos take precedence over these defaults.

Final project assets (JPEG exports of the generated originals):

- `app/assets/hospitals/clinic-exterior.jpg`
- `app/assets/hospitals/clinic-interior.jpg`

The original generated images remain under the generator's default output directory. The JPEG assets are bundled locally, prefetched, and used for listing covers and the horizontal hospital gallery.

## Exterior prompt

Create a polished landscape architectural photograph for an illustrative fictional clinic listing in a hospital booking app. A small modern urban family clinic in Mumbai: inviting glass ground-floor entrance, clean off-white stone facade, subtle warm wood detail, green planting, quiet Indian city street context, natural bright daylight, realistic materials and proportions. No people, no medical claims, no words, no signage, no logos, no watermarks, no collage. Editorial architectural photography, landscape wide 3:2 composition, attractive but plausible modest clinic. Intended as a bundled placeholder photo for a fictional seeded clinic, not an actual healthcare provider.

## Interior prompt

Create a polished landscape interior architectural photograph for an illustrative fictional clinic gallery in a hospital booking app. A contemporary Indian outpatient clinic reception and waiting area: daylight through large windows, white walls, muted sage upholstered seating, pale oak reception counter, a few plants, spotless practical interior, realistic modest scale and coherent furniture. No people, no words, no signage, no logos, no watermarks, no collage. Editorial architectural photography, 3:2 landscape composition. Intended as a bundled placeholder photo for a fictional seeded clinic, not an actual healthcare provider.
